# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period
are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Employees on a
performance improvement plan (PIP) are not eligible for remote work until
the plan is closed out.

## Core Hours

All remote employees must be reachable on Slack and video calls between
10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone. This is referred to
internally as "core hours." Meetings that span multiple time zones should
be scheduled inside the overlapping core hours window whenever possible.

## Equipment

Acme provides a laptop, a monitor, and a $300 one-time home office stipend
for remote employees. The stipend can be used for a desk, chair, or
peripherals, and must be claimed within 60 days of a remote work request
being approved. Equipment remains company property and must be returned
within 14 days of an employee's last day.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal tools
from outside the office network. Personal devices are not permitted to
access production systems under any circumstances. Lost or stolen
equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

## Approval Process

Remote work requests are submitted through the HR portal and require
sign-off from the employee's direct manager. Requests are typically
processed within 5 business days. Denials must include a written reason
and can be appealed once to the department head.
