Nimbus Analytics — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What data sources does Nimbus support?
A: Nimbus connects natively to PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, and BigQuery.
Support for MongoDB is currently in private beta and can be requested
through your account manager.

Q: How is usage billed?
A: Billing is based on monthly active queries (MAQ), not seats. The
Starter plan includes 10,000 MAQ per month at $0.002 per query beyond
that. The Enterprise plan negotiates a flat rate and does not meter
individual queries.

Q: What is the data retention policy?
A: Query logs are retained for 90 days on the Starter plan and 2 years on
the Enterprise plan. Underlying customer data is never copied into
Nimbus; Nimbus only stores query metadata and cached result sets, which
expire after 24 hours by default.

Q: Can I self-host Nimbus?
A: Self-hosted deployments are only available on the Enterprise plan and
require Kubernetes 1.27 or later. Nimbus Cloud (the default) requires no
infrastructure from the customer.

Q: What happens if I exceed my plan's rate limit?
A: Requests beyond the plan's rate limit are queued for up to 30 seconds
and then rejected with a 429 status code. Enterprise customers can request
a temporary rate limit increase through support for planned traffic
spikes, such as a product launch.

Q: How do I rotate my API key?
A: API keys can be rotated from Settings > API Keys in the dashboard. The
old key remains valid for 24 hours after a new one is issued, to avoid
downtime during rollout.
